# Charts and Interpretation

The four PNG outputs under [artifacts/charts](../artifacts/charts) were generated by `app/services/charts.py` from the synthetic SQLite demo records on 2026-10-29. The live analytics page regenerates them from the selected database period.

| Chart | Source and calculation | Axes / units | Interpretation and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `demo_expense_distribution.png` | October expenses grouped by category | Category share; percent | Shows where October recorded expenses were concentrated. It reflects entered transactions only. |
| `demo_income_expenses.png` | All records pivoted by calendar month and type | Month, INR | Lets the user compare monthly inflow and outflow. Missing months remain absent. |
| `demo_savings_trend.png` | Monthly income minus monthly expense | Month, INR | Shows direction and size of recorded savings. Zero income does not imply a useful savings rate. |
| `demo_budget_actual.png` | October budgets joined to actual category expenses | Category, INR | Compares planned allocations to actuals; Grocery spending exceeded its ₹5,000 demo allocation. Unbudgeted spend appears in the service output, but has no planned bar. |

All chart titles name the subject, monetary charts label INR, and legends distinguish series. Empty-data handling creates an explanatory no-data image instead of a fabricated chart.
